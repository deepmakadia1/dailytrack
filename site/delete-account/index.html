Delete your DailyTrack account and data

DailyTrack by Midexcode · Last updated: 29 October 2026

Your DailyTrack account is your Google sign-in. We do not run a server that stores your entries: they live on your phone and, if you back up, in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Deleting your account means deleting those backups, signing out and removing the app's access to your Google account. You can do all of this yourself in a few minutes, or ask us to help.

Steps to delete your account in the app

  1. Open DailyTrack and go to More → Backup and restore.
  2. Tap your email address (Manage your account) to open the Account screen.
  3. Tap Delete my backups and confirm. This deletes every DailyTrack backup from your Google Drive.
  4. Tap Sign out and confirm.
  5. To delete the data on your phone too, go to More → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  6. Remove DailyTrack's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions (select DailyTrack, then Delete all connections).

No longer have the app?

You can still delete your data without installing DailyTrack:

  • Drive backups: open drive.google.com on a computer, go to Settings → Manage apps, find DailyTrack, then choose Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Google access: remove DailyTrack at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask us: email support@midexcode.com with the subject "DailyTrack account deletion request", sent from the Google account you used in the app. We will confirm within 30 days and delete anything we hold about you, such as your support emails. Because your backups are in your own Drive, we cannot open or delete them for you, so we will also send you the steps above.
